\# Meridian Dental Co. — Brand Brief



\## Positioning

Premium dental clinic in Indiranagar, Bengaluru.

Vibe: hotel lobby, not hospital. Feels like booking a spa, not a checkup.

Target customer: 28–45, professionals, household income ₹15L+.



\## Tagline

Dentistry, considered.



\## Services (3 hero offerings)

1\. Aesthetic Dentistry — veneers, whitening, smile design

2\. Restorative Care — implants, crowns, full-mouth rehabilitation

3\. Preventive Wellness — cleanings, checkups, oral health planning



\## Visual Identity

\- Primary: #0A0A0A (matte black)

\- Accent: #B8956A (brushed brass)

\- Background: #F5F2ED (warm off-white)



\## Typography

\- Headings: Fraunces (serif, editorial weight)

\- Body: Inter (sans-serif, neutral)



\## Tone

Quiet confidence. No exclamation marks. No "transform your smile!" 

energy. Think Aesop or Le Labo, not Colgate.
